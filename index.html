Clinical Learning, Assessment & Review Assistant

Helping nursing educators assess clinical skills more consistently and at greater scale

CLARA is a Purdue research project that uses authentic student skill-validation videos and expert annotations to support step-level clinical skills assessment—and to build toward explainable, actionable feedback for learners.

Explore the tool Meet the team

Why this matters

Faculty time and individualized feedback are hard to scale

Clinical skills “checkoffs” require one-on-one competency validation. That takes substantial instructor time and expert judgment—especially for subtle errors such as sterile-field breaches—so programs face a tradeoff between how many students they can train and how carefully each performance is reviewed. CLARA aims to support educators with structured, expert-grounded assessment tools so learners can get clearer practice feedback without proportionally increasing faculty workload.

Our approach

Step-level scoring beyond pass / fail

Experts annotate each procedural step across three dimensions so evaluation can capture accuracy, execution quality, and context—not only whether an action occurred.

Correctness

Whether each step is performed accurately according to clinical standards.

Performance

How effectively and smoothly each step is executed.

Allowance

External factors that may affect task execution (contextual difficulty).

Intended learner-facing functions

Research goals Examiner, Personal Coach, and Learning Diary describe the project’s intended continuous-learning cycle. The working system today is the expert annotation tool below; automated student-facing assessment is planned next-step work.

  • 02

Inside the tool

Existing Expert annotation workspace

Clinical partners and annotators use CLARA’s nursing video annotation system to review authentic skill-validation recordings and build expert-grounded ratings.

  1. Select a procedure video Choose a skill (for example, Foley catheter) and open a specific validation recording from the procedures menu.
  2. Mark procedural steps Play the video, navigate step by step, and mark events such as sterile breaches or allowance periods on the timeline.
  3. Add ratings and rationales Score correctness and performance for each recorded action, flag ambiguous cases, and save the expert review.

Progress and next steps

What we have built so far

  • Built a nursing video dataset of authentic procedural performances and errors, plus an expert annotation platform and trained procedural assessment models.
  • Partnered with Purdue School of Nursing to collect skill-validation videos and establish expert-grounded assessment standards.
  • Published preliminary research on video-language models for AI-assisted nursing skills assessment (arXiv:2509.16810).

What’s next

Next we plan to turn the annotation tool and models into an automated analysis pipeline, add a student-facing portal for practice feedback, and run supervised pilots with nursing students and instructors at Purdue Nursing and NCNC—followed by clinical validation against expert assessments.
