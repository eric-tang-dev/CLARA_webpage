Clinical Learning and Assessment Reasoning Agent

Helping nursing students improve, one clinical skill at a time

CLARA is a Purdue research project developing AI tools to help nursing educators review clinical skills and give students more detailed feedback. Our work combines videos of students practicing nursing procedures with assessments from clinical experts.

Explore the tool Meet the team

Why this matters

Every student needs feedback. Instructor time is limited.

Reviewing a nursing procedure takes time and close attention. Instructors need to assess each step, notice subtle mistakes, and explain what a student can improve. CLARA aims to support this work so students have more opportunities to learn from practice.

Our approach

Looking beyond pass or fail

A completed step does not tell the whole story. Our annotation framework asks clinical experts to consider whether a step was correct, how well it was performed, and what circumstances affected it.

Correctness

Was the step performed correctly?

Performance

How well was the step performed?

Allowance

What external conditions affected the student’s performance?

Inside the tool

Inside CLARA’s annotation tool

Our annotation tool lets clinical experts review nursing skills videos and record detailed assessments of each procedural step. These reviews help us build the data and assessment standards used in our AI research.

  1. Choose a video Open a nursing skill and select a student practice recording.
  2. Review and mark procedural steps Watch the video, move through each step, and mark events such as sterile breaches or allowance periods.
  3. Add ratings and explanations Score correctness and performance, note why, and save the review.

Progress and next steps

What we have built so far

  • We collected nursing skills videos that capture real student performances and common procedural errors.
  • We built an annotation tool so clinical experts can review those videos and record detailed assessments.
  • We trained models for procedural assessment and published preliminary research on video-language models for AI-assisted nursing skills assessment (arXiv:2509.16810).
  • We collaborate with Purdue School of Nursing to collect practice videos and develop assessment standards.

What’s next

We plan to connect our annotation tools and AI models in an automated video-review workflow, develop a student portal for practice feedback, and test the system with nursing students and instructors. We will compare its assessments with expert reviews to understand where it performs well and where it needs improvement.
